# Claimable skills — allowlist

`experiences.md` holds the canonical **bullets** — things done, with evidence.
This file holds skills you can honestly claim on a resume or in a skills field
**even though no bullet spells them out**: a language you know but never
shipped in production, a tool you have used but not written about.

Agents may use anything here when tailoring. Anything NOT here and NOT in
`experiences.md` must not be written down. That is a hard boundary, not a
guideline — it is what makes the tailored output defensible.

Add a line whenever you confirm a new one. Keep the note: it tells a future
agent how far the claim reasonably stretches, which is the part that stops a
"language proficiency" from becoming "5 years of production experience".
