# Experiences — canonical bullets

Everything you have actually done, in the words you are willing to defend in
an interview. Both agents read this file, and **nothing may appear on a resume
or in a form that is not traceable to a line here** or to
`claimable_skills.md`. That constraint is the whole point: it is what stops a
tailoring pass from quietly inventing an achievement.

When you finish a project, internship, or paper, add the bullet here first;
deciding whether it belongs on the resume is a separate step.
