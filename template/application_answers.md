# Application answer sheet

Standard answers for the repetitive parts of application forms. The fill
assistant reads this before filling anything, so the same question never gets
asked twice — and so your answers stay consistent across a hundred forms,
which matters more than it sounds when the same recruiter sees two of them.

Facts live in `experiences.md` (identity, address, schools, employers); this
file is only for **form questions and how to answer them**.

The question list below is the reusable part — US application forms ask a
remarkably fixed set. Fill in your own answers; delete rows that don't apply.

---

## Work authorization — the two questions almost every US form asks

| Question (wording varies) | Answer |
|---|---|
| Are you legally eligible / authorized to work in the country of employment? | `<Yes/No>` |
| Do you now, or will you in the future, require sponsorship for work authorization? | `<Yes/No>` |

**Why it's worth writing down:** these two are asked separately and mean
different things, and the honest answers can look contradictory to someone
skimming ("yes I can work now" + "yes I'll need sponsorship later"). Write
your reasoning here once.

**Never soften an answer to get past a filter.** A false answer here is
grounds for a rescinded offer, and the fill assistant is instructed to
refuse it.
